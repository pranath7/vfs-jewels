Ring & Kada Sizing Guide

How do I know my correct Kada or Bangle size (2.4, 2.6, 2.8)?

You can easily measure an existing well-fitting bangle using a standard ruler across its inner diameter:

  • Size 2.4 (Small): Inner diameter of 2.25 inches (57 mm)
  • Size 2.6 (Medium — Most Common): Inner diameter of 2.375 inches (60 mm)
  • Size 2.8 (Large): Inner diameter of 2.5 inches (64 mm)

Many of our kadas also come with an openable lock clasp or free-size open-cuff design that comfortably fits all wrists.

Are your rings fixed size or adjustable?

Over 80% of our cocktail and solitaire CZ rings feature an invisible comfort-fit adjustable band that smoothly adjusts between Indian ring sizes 6 through 10. For fixed-size rings, specific measurements are explicitly mentioned in the title and description.

Shipping & Pan-India Delivery

How long does delivery take and which couriers do you use?

All orders are packed and dispatched directly from our Sowcarpet, Chennai fulfillment hub:

  • Tamil Nadu & South India: 1 to 2 business days (via ST Courier / DTDC Express).
  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad): 2 to 4 business days (via FedEx / DTDC Air).
  • Rest of India: 3 to 5 business days.

You will receive an automated tracking link & parcel photo slip on WhatsApp as soon as your parcel is handed to the courier.
