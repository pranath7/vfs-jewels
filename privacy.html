  • To process, pack, and courier your jewelry via trusted regional carriers (DTDC, ST Courier, FedEx).
  • To send automated WhatsApp delivery tracking updates, photo slips, and tax invoices.
  • To handle 18K Anti-Tarnish warranty claims, sizing replacements, and customer service.
  • To prevent fraudulent orders and maintain platform security.

We retain order history and tax records for a statutory minimum of 7 years as mandated by Indian GST and commercial taxation laws. Anonymized session telemetry is deleted every 90 days.

You retain the following rights regarding your personal data:
